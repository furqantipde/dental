About Ammar Dental Surgery

Dedicated to Giving You a Healthier, Brighter Smile in Hafizabad

Welcome to Ammar Dental Surgery (Centre of Smiles), your trusted destination for comprehensive dental care and aesthetic transformations in Hafizabad, Pakistan. We pride ourselves on delivering top-notch dental treatments in a comfortable, friendly, and pain-free environment.

Whether you need routine scaling & polishing, advanced root canal treatments, dazzling teeth whitening, dental implants, or smile design makeovers, our experienced dental surgeons utilize modern techniques and equipment to ensure optimal outcomes.

Our Specialized Treatments

From routine dental cleaning to advanced restorative and aesthetic dental procedures, we provide full-spectrum care under strict sterilization guidelines.

Teeth Scaling & Polishing

Remove stubborn plaque, tartar, and surface stains to restore your natural gum health and bright smile.

Teeth Whitening

Advanced professional whitening procedures to safely lighten your teeth by shades in just one visit.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement solutions that restore normal biting function and appearance.

Crowns & Bridges

High-grade durable crowns and dental bridges to protect weakened teeth and fill missing gaps seamlessly.

Dentures (CD / RPD)

Root Canal Treatments

Aesthetic Procedures

Smile makeovers, teeth reshaping, gum contouring, and aesthetic procedures to maximize your confidence.
